Coming home to Abuja? How to book a shortlet you have never seen

By the Prestine Apartments team. Published 28 September 2026, 3 minute read.

The short answer

Confirm the address, see the actual unit, get the full price in writing, and pay only through a channel that sends you a written confirmation. Below are the seven checks in full, the questions to send the host, and what to expect when you land.

Seven checks before you pay

  1. Find out who owns or manages the place. You want a business or a person you can name and call back.
  2. Ask for a video of the exact unit you will get. Photos can be old or borrowed. A video filmed this week cannot.
  3. Check the address on Google Maps. The street, the building and the landmarks around it should match what the host told you.
  4. Get the full nightly price in writing. Ask what it includes, so tax and service charge do not appear at check-in.
  5. Pay only through a channel that sends a written confirmation. If anything goes wrong, that confirmation is your proof.
  6. Avoid large transfers through online-only banks. Police investigators say that money is hard to trace once it has gone.
  7. Walk away from a host who rushes you or cannot show the unit. A host who needs your money today is a warning sign.

In October 2025 a diaspora family lost ₦1.3m to an Instagram "agent" for a shortlet in Lagos. That case was in Lagos, but the checks work the same in Abuja.

Questions to ask the host on WhatsApp

Copy these into your first message:

  • What is the exact address?
  • Can you send a video of the unit I will get?
  • What does the nightly price include?
  • What time is check-in, and what time is check-out?
  • What happens if the power goes?
  • How do I get from the airport to you?

Landing before dawn

If you fly British Airways from London, flight BA83 is due into Abuja at 04:50, so you land before 5am. Plan the ride before you board. Uber shut down in Nigeria on 2 September 2026. Bolt is cleared to work at the airport again, and the official airport taxi fares are shown through FAAN's ACHRAMS system. The airport is about 40 km from the Central Business District, 37 to 45 minutes by road. Send your host your flight time so someone knows when to expect you.
