In Abuja for a week or more on work: hotel or serviced apartment?

By the Prestine Apartments team. Published 28 September 2026, 2 minute read.

The short answer

For a night or two, a hotel is simpler. For a longer stay, an apartment gives you a kitchen, laundry and a separate room to work in. Which one costs less depends on the rooms you compare, so we have put real Abuja prices side by side below and let you do the sum for your own trip.

When you price a hotel room for the same dates, check whether it has a kitchen and somewhere to wash clothes. On a trip of two weeks or more, those two decide how much you spend on food and laundry.

The Premium Luxury One-Bedroom is set up for "both short and extended stays", with a separate living room.

Our Apo apartments are about 14 km from the Central Business District, 15 to 25 minutes off-peak. Read what staying in Apo is like before you decide.

When a hotel is the better call

For one or two nights, a hotel saves you the planning. If your meetings are at a hotel venue, staying in the same building saves the drive. And if you want a restaurant on site, pick a hotel, because our apartments are built around your own kitchen.
